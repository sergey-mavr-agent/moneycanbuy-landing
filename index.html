No. Log in with Telegram or email; the app makes you a wallet, no seed phrase.

What if someone fakes it?

No. It's locked in the contract until the dare is done or expires.

    Bonds, fees & edge cases

    Bonds: 0.5 IMD + 3% (doer) or + 1% (dispute) of the author's own reward, in the reward currency; never doubled. The 10% fee is taken only on payout. 5% of the reward goes to the NFT votes on the winning side if the vote decided right, otherwise to the treasury (to burn for a staked doer). A doer with a $YOLO stake gets up to 4% of the reward back from the fee. Loser's bond: 50% to the winning side, 50% to the correct NFT votes; 100% to the winner if the swarm overturned the vote or the vote did not decide. No vote and no swarm call: the submission is accepted. Demo shows a doer without a stake; $ values use example prices.
